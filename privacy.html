Resume Builder

Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-23

Your resume stays in your browser

Resume Builder has no account and no backend. What you type is kept in your browser's localStorage on this site, so your draft is still there when you come back. It is not uploaded anywhere.

The Save button keeps a copy in your browser and downloads it to you as a JSON file. (It first tries a save endpoint that only exists when the app runs on the developer's own machine; on this site there is no such server, so that attempt simply fails.)

Downloads are made on your device

The image and PDF downloads are generated in your browser with html2canvas and jsPDF, which are bundled with the site. Your resume is not sent to a server to be converted.

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics 4, loaded from googletagmanager.com, to count usage: page views (Templates, Builder), which template was picked or previewed, which download format was used, and when a premium PDF option was unlocked. It never sends resume content, file names or promo codes. Google may set its own cookies for this; you can block them with your browser or a content blocker and the app keeps working.

Other third-party requests

None. Fonts, scripts and styles are served from this site; there is no CDN. Links you choose to follow (for example the WhatsApp link for asking about a promo code) take you to those services.

Payments and promo codes

There are no payments on this site. Some premium PDF options can be unlocked with a promo code; the code is checked in your browser and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Everything is in your browser, so you delete it there: clear this site's data (in most browsers, Settings → Privacy → Site data, or the padlock icon in the address bar → Site settings → Delete data). This removes your draft, saved copies and theme choice. Downloaded files stay wherever you saved them.
